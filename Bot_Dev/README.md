### Description
Fitcord relies on a two-prong approach to implementing its vision. Because of this, development is split along two primary directories - Bot Development and Mobile App Development.
Both sides of this platform tie to the same shared DB so information is universal across the divide.

### Discord Bot
This sub-directory facilitates the development of the Discord bot. Here, the logic will be developed for:
- Tracking and administering challenges
- Account verification
- Sharing workout information
- And more

### Proposed Development Notes
- The bot may be coded in Python to leverage the community support for Python-based development for Discord bots
