### Description
Fitcord is for Gen Z users who want to stay consistent with their fitness goals while remaining active in their existing online communities who need motivation, accountability, and seamless integration between fitness tracking while maintaining their existing social bubbles. Fitcord is a social-integrated fitness platform that connects workout tracking, challenges, and progress sharing directly into Discord communities through a companion mobile app. Unlike traditional fitness apps that require users to build new habits and isolated communities within a separate platform Fitcord brings fitness into existing social ecosystems, enabling consistency through familiar communities, shared goals, and continuous social reinforcement.
